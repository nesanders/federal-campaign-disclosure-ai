Regional Organizing Director

Reports to: Political/Organizing Director 

Location: Manchester, New Hampshire (extensive in-district travel required).
Employment Type: Full-time for the duration of the primary campaign.

Role Summary: The Regional Organizing Director is a core member of the campaign’s grassroots operation, responsible for executing day-to-day voter contact and volunteer organizing across New Hampshire’s First Congressional District. This role is designed to build, mobilize, and sustain a statewide field program through phone banks, postcards, canvassing, and county-level coordination. This role requires ability to work evenings and weekends as needed.

Key Responsibilities and Deliverables:

  • Recruit, train and manage volunteers in your region to execute a voter persuasion and ID program. 
  • Execute weekly phone banks and canvasses.
  • Distribute call lists, scripts, and canvassing materials; ensure volunteers are prepared and supported.
  • Conduct follow-up outreach to deepen volunteer engagement and development.
  • Maintain regular communication with core supporters.
  • Support execution of local organizing plans, events, and outreach efforts.
  • Track volunteer participation, voter contact metrics, and organizing activity.
  • Maintain accurate and up-to-date records in campaign databases.
  • Collaborate with the communications and digital teams to amplify campaign support, events, and organizing efforts.
  • Work closely with political, communications, and digital teams to align organizing efforts.

What We Value for This Position:

  • Tireless work ethic and flexibility to work evenings and weekends as needed.
  • Willingness to travel extensively throughout New Hampshire.
  • Strong ethical standards, professionalism, and commitment to confidentiality.
  • Excellent time management and ability to work independently and collaboratively.
  • Positive, can-do attitude with a genuine passion for public service and campaign work.
  • Respectful and collegial demeanor.
  • Commitment to Democratic politics, the values, vision, and goals of the campaign.

Experience and Skills:

  • **Required:Valid driver’s license, reliable transportation, and ability to travel across the state.
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills, with the ability to build and maintain relationships with diverse stakeholders.
  • Strong organizational and research skills with exceptional attention to detail.
  • Proficiency with Google Suite and familiarity with campaign tools (NGP/VAN or similar systems) a plus, not a requirement.
  • Experience in digital organizing and digital content creation is a plus, but not required.
  • Experience in political campaigns, advocacy, or community organizing (internships or campus organizing experience welcome) preferred.
  • Willingness to work long hours.

Compensation:

Salary: $4,500 per month, commensurate with experience. The campaign offers a monthly healthcare stipend. 

This is a full-time position for the duration of the campaign. Looking for candidates to start immediately. Interested applicants should send a resume and letter of interest to Political/Organizing Director, Nick Sperduto by April 30: [email protected]
